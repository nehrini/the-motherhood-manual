I'm a first-time mum living in Kildare. My son was born by C-section at the Rotunda, and even with private care, wonderful midwives and two master's degrees behind me, the first weeks of motherhood left me feeling completely lost.

The information new parents need in Ireland does exist. The trouble is that it's scattered across booklets, websites, apps and well-meaning advice, it often contradicts itself, and it arrives when you're at your most exhausted. With no family in Ireland, my husband and I were piecing it all together ourselves, usually at 3am.

**The Motherhood Manual** is where I'm writing that experience down honestly: the overwhelm, the conflicting advice, the Irish-specific things nobody explained, and the small moments that got us through.

If you're in the thick of it right now: you're not alone, and it isn't because you're doing it wrong.
