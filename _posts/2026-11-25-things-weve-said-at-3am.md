Some nights, the only thing keeping us going is each other. And, it turns out, the absolute nonsense we say to each other. These are all real. We have the dark circles to prove it.

## 11:52pm

**Me:** "Your turn."

**Him:** "I did the last one."

**Me:** "That was yesterday."

**Him:** "It was forty minutes ago."

## 12:30am

**Him:** "Just tap me if you need me."

*I have never once tapped him. I have thought about it. Violently.*

## 1:05am

**Him:** "What's the room temperature?"

**Me:** "Same as when you asked four minutes ago."

## 1:18am

**Me:** "I think it's the babygro. Last night he slept, and he was wearing the white one with cars on it. This one has trains."

## 1:40am

**Him:** "Did you log that feed?"

**Me:** "In my head, yes."

## 2:02am

**Me:** "Is that a hungry cry or a tired cry?"

**Him:** "It's a cry cry."

## 2:15am

**Him:** "Have you tried turning him off and on again?"

## 2:31am

**Me:** "If you take this burp and settle, I'll do the 6am. And the nappy. And make you coffee."

## 2:47am

**Him:** "Is he awake?"

**Me:** "His eyes are open, but I don't know if he's *awake* awake."

## 3:04am

**Me:** "Why is he so happy? It's 3am. Who is he talking to?"

## 3:12am

*Whispering:* "Don't make eye contact. He can sense it."

## 3:26am

**Me:** "Shh, shh, shh…"

**Him:** "I'm not the baby."

**Me:** "Sorry. Reflex."

## 3:41am

**Him:** "Where's the baby?"

**Me:** "…You're holding him."

## 3:58am

**Me:** "Why is the dog bringing him her toy again?"

**Him:** "She's helping."

**Me:** "Is she, though?"

## 4:09am

**Him:** "She's sniffing his nappy again."

**Me:** "No need. I can smell him from here."

## 4:15am

**Me:** "Did the dog just sigh at us?"

## 4:33am

**Me:** "Did he just smile at me or was that wind?"

**Him:** "Take the smile."

## 4:50am

**Him:** "He's asleep. Don't move. Don't breathe."

*Neither of us moved for 45 minutes.*

## 5:36am

**Me:** "We made him."

**Him:** "I know."

**Me:** "He's perfect."

**Him:** "I know."

*The baby immediately woke up.*

## 5:52am

**Me:** "What day is it?"

**Him:** "Night."

## 6:10am

**Me:** "I put the coffee pod in the dishwasher."

**Him:** "Why?"

**Me:** "I don't know."

## 6:14am

**Him:** "Are we the adults in the room?"

**Me:** "…Apparently."
